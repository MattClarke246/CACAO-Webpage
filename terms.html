2. Nonprofit Purpose & Educational Mission

CACAO is an Alabama-registered 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization established in 2011. Our mission is to promote Caribbean cultural awareness, foster community unity in Central Alabama, and raise scholarship funds for Caribbean American students pursuing higher education.

3. Scholarship Program Terms

  • Eligibility & Review: Scholarship guidelines, eligibility criteria, and deadlines are published annually on our website and application materials. Applications are reviewed independently by the CACAO Scholarship Committee.
  • No Guarantee: Submitting an application does not guarantee selection or financial award. Awards are subject to annual fundraising availability and committee discretion.
  • Accuracy of Information: Applicants certify that all information submitted in scholarship applications is accurate, truthful, and complete.
